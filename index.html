Očisti Kontakte Duplikati

Find and merge duplicate contacts on your iPhone — with a local backup before every merge.

OcistiKontakte is a Croatian-language contact cleaner for iOS by Cosmic Production d.o.o. It scans your address book on-device, shows a preview of every suggested merge, and saves a local backup before any change. Your contacts never leave your device.
